About

Experienced Comms Pro Across Pro Sports & College Athletics

I’m Daniel Loftus, a Denver-based sports writer and communicator. I help teams, athletic departments, athletes and sponsors turn their stories into fan engagement, marketing opportunities and revenue.

I spent three years in the Brooklyn Nets’ communications department, writing the releases, guides and game notes behind a pro franchise’s media operation and planning storytelling opportunities for athletes, coaches and executives. I also built a media platform that drew 10,000+ visits in its first five months. Before that, I worked in athletic communications at Notre Dame, Syracuse and Xavier and supported the Big Ten Conference and the NFL.

I hold a master’s in management from Notre Dame’s Mendoza College of Business and a bachelor’s in public relations from Syracuse’s Newhouse School.
